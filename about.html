About the Company

A small studio building audio that’s worth keeping.

Antonio’s Airpods+ is an independent audio brand designing wireless earbuds, headphones, audio pods, and bluetooth speakers for listeners who prefer fewer, better things.

Our story.

Antonio’s Airpods+ was founded on a simple frustration: most wireless audio either sounded cheap, looked disposable, or was priced like jewellery without the craft to match. We wanted a fourth option.

We started as a two-person project in 2023 — a pair of friends, a borrowed studio, and an obsession with the way a pair of headphones feels in your hands on the tenth day you own them, not the first. Today, we still design every product in-house, prototype locally, and only release a new model when it actually improves on the last one.

We don’t run seasonal drops. We don’t chase trends. We’d rather ship a short catalogue of pieces we’re proud of — the kind you forget you’re wearing on a long-haul flight, and remember fondly three years later when the battery is still going.

Every pair is tuned against reference recordings our engineering lead has been using since conservatory. Every shell is finished in materials we’d happily keep on our own desks. And every order is backed by a two-year warranty because we think that should be the minimum, not a luxury upgrade.

Thank you for listening.
